// ABOUT THE FEST

Where Code Meets Creativity

Nexus '26 is the premier annual technical festival organized by the students and faculty of the Institute of Technology. Designed by developers, for developers, this fest brings together brilliant minds from across the country to compete, build, and network.

Whether you are a master of frontend frameworks, a machine learning wizard, a retro gamer, or a robotics fanatic, Nexus has a stage built just for you. Expect intense hackathons, mind-bending quizzes, and keynote sessions from industry leaders.
